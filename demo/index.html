LedgerFM transaction risk demo

LedgerFM is an open foundation model for transaction sequences. It reads each account's history and scores every transaction. Everything runs in your browser: your file never leaves your device.

Ready.

The 50 highest-risk transactions. Each score uses only that account's earlier transactions.

CSV format: one row per transaction.
  • Required: txn_id, ts (e.g. 2024-01-31 13:45:00), account (card or customer), counterparty (merchant or receiver), amount.
  • Optional: currency, category, channel, location, src_bank, dst_bank, currency_recv, amount_recv, errors, and label (1 = fraud) to see how well the score ranks it.
  • Up to 20,000 rows. For more, use pip install ledgerfm.

⚠️ The risk score comes from a LedgerFM model fine-tuned on synthetic US card fraud (Sparkov). It is a demonstration, not calibrated for other payment types. On your own data, fine-tune LedgerFM or add its embeddings to your model. The example cards come from Sparkov's test period (late Aug–Dec 2020), which the model never saw. Models are int8-quantised ONNX (risk score within 0.007 of the PyTorch model).
